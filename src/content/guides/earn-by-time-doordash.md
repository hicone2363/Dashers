## Quick Answer

Earn by Time is an optional DoorDash earning mode where you receive a minimum guaranteed hourly rate for **active time only**, plus 100% of tips. Active time starts when you accept an offer and ends when you complete it. Time spent waiting for offers does not count. The hourly rate varies by location and time of day, and the mode is available only in select US cities.

## How Earn by Time Works

When starting a shift in an eligible area, the Dasher app prompts you to choose between **Earn per Offer** and **Earn by Time** (indicated by an hourglass icon).

Once in Earn by Time mode:
1. Each incoming offer displays the estimated total mileage and a target deliver-by time.
2. Instead of a fixed per-order base pay, compensation accrues dynamically based on the exact active minutes from acceptance to drop-off.
3. 100% of customer tips are added on top of your accrued active-time earnings.

## Active Time vs Dash Time vs Idle Time

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Dash time | Everything between starting and ending your dash |
| Active time | From accepting an offer until it is completed or canceled, including waiting at the merchant |
| Idle time | Time spent waiting for an offer |

Active time + idle time = dash time. Only active time is covered by the guaranteed rate.

**Illustration (hypothetical numbers, not real DoorDash rates):** if your guaranteed rate were $15 per active hour and an offer took 8 active minutes, the base pay would be about $2.00 before tips. Check the rate shown in your own app.

## Rules You Need to Know

- **Decline limit:** up to one declined or unassigned offer per hour.
- **Timing matters:** repeated time-related violations can lock the mode. While it is locked you can still earn per offer.
- **Priority access:** not available in this mode, though some high-paying offers remain.
- **Availability:** select US cities only.

## Earn by Time vs Earn per Offer

| Feature | Earn by Time | Earn per Offer |
|---|---|---|
| Base pay | Guaranteed hourly rate for active time | Set per offer (time, distance, desirability) |
| Idle time | Not paid | Not paid |
| Tips | 100% to you | 100% to you |
| Declining | Limited to one per hour | You choose per offer |
| Peak Pay | Adds to the hourly rate when available | Adds an amount per eligible delivery |

*Note on tips: Customers can tip on orders in both modes, though offer transparency and customer tipping behavior vary across individual orders.*

## When Earn by Time May Make Sense

Whether this mode pays off depends on your market, your hours, and the offers you tend to get. DoorDash does not publish a single "best" situation, so use these questions and test them against your own numbers.

### Four questions to ask yourself

1. **How much do you wait at merchants?** Wait time at the restaurant counts as active time in Earn by Time. If long waits are common where you dash, compare how that time is treated in each mode.
2. **How often are you idle?** Time spent waiting for an offer is not covered by the guaranteed rate. Slow areas may still leave you with long unpaid gaps.
3. **How far do you drive after drop-off?** According to DoorDash's definition, active time ends when the delivery is completed, so the drive back toward busy areas is not part of active time. Check each drop-off location before using your one decline per hour.
4. **What rate does your app show?** The guaranteed rate varies by location and time of day. Compare it to your own recent earnings per hour in Earn per Offer.

## Troubleshooting: Earn by Time Not Showing Up

If you do not see the option to select Earn by Time, consider these officially documented causes:

- **City availability:** DoorDash states the mode is available only in select US markets. Not all regions or zones offer it.
- **Account status:** Repeated time-related violations or excessive delays can lock access to Earn by Time. While locked, you can still deliver using Earn per Offer.
- **App updates:** Ensure you are using the most recent version of the Dasher app with location permissions set to "Always Allow" while dashing.
- **Zone capacity:** During certain shifts, DoorDash may limit the number of couriers active on Earn by Time simultaneously.
